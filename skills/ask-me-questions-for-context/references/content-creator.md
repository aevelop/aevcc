# Content Creator — Question Set

For YouTubers, podcasters, short-form creators, and anyone making content at a regular cadence. The output file helps Claude Code with ideation, scripting, repurposing, and content decisions that fit *this* creator's channel — not generic creator advice.

## Themes to cover (40-50 questions, one at a time)

### 1. What and where (3-4)
- What do you make? Where do you publish it?
- How long have you been doing it? How often do you publish?
- What's your current production cadence — and what's actually sustainable?

### 2. Audience reality (4-5) — separate "who they say they want" from "who actually shows up"
- Who do you *think* your audience is?
- Now: who actually shows up in comments, replies, DMs?
- What do they say that surprises you?
- What does your highest-performing recent piece tell you about who's really watching?

### 3. Content pillars (3-5)
- What are the 3-5 topics you keep returning to?
- Which pillar performs best? Which underperforms?
- What pillar are you secretly tired of but feel like you have to keep making?

### 4. On-camera vs off-camera self (3-5)
- Describe your on-camera (or on-mic) persona in 3 phrases.
- Describe yourself off-camera in 3 phrases. Where do they differ?
- Is the gap healthy or is it costing you something?

### 5. Wins (3-5) — demand specifics
- Pick the piece you're most proud of. Why did it work? (Not "the algorithm" — what was good about it?)
- What's a piece that performed badly but you still love?
- What's a moment in a video/episode you'd put on a highlight reel?

### 6. Flops (3-5)
- A piece you made that flopped — what went wrong, in your gut?
- What's a recurring failure mode? (Bad thumbnails? Buried hook? Wrong topic?)
- What did you stop doing after a flop taught you something?

### 7. Trend rejections (5-7) — pursue hard, this is gold
- What trend in your niche would you never follow, even if it'd probably perform?
- What style of video/post/episode do you refuse to make?
- What's a format everyone in your space is doing that you actively avoid?
- What kind of thumbnail would you never use?
- What clickbait pattern do you refuse to deploy?

### 8. Production constraints (4-5)
- Solo or team? Who does what?
- What's your real production budget — time, money, gear?
- What's a constraint you've turned into part of your style?
- What's a constraint that's actively limiting you?

### 9. Creators you steal from (3-4)
- Three creators you admire. For each: what specifically would you take? (Not "their vibe" — a *specific* technique, structural choice, or move.)
- A creator whose audience you'd want to inherit. Why?

### 10. Audience effect (3-4)
- After someone watches your stuff, what do you want them to feel, think, or do?
- What's a comment that made you go "yes, *that* is what I'm doing here"?
- What do people misunderstand about what you're trying to make?

### 11. Hard nos (5-7)
- What would you never make, even if someone paid you?
- What sponsors would you turn down?
- What positions, takes, or hot takes are off-limits?
- What's a topic you're tempted by but refuse to touch?

### 12. Repurposing (2-3)
- Where does the original piece usually live, and what gets repurposed to what?
- What does NOT translate well from your long-form to short-form (or vice versa)?

## Specific things to push on

- **"I want to be authentic"** → "What does inauthentic content look like for you? Name a creator who does the version you'd never do."
- **"I want to grow"** → "Grow into what? Pick a creator at the size you want. What about them is the model — and what about them isn't?"
- **"My audience likes [X]"** → "How do you know? Comments, retention, DMs?"
- **Generic creator advice** → "That's any YouTuber/podcaster. What's specific about *your* channel?"

## File output structure for this variant

```markdown
# Content Identity for [Name / Channel]

## What I make and where
## Who actually watches/listens (not who I wished did)
## My content pillars
## My on-camera persona vs my off-camera self
## What works (with specific examples)
## What doesn't work — recurring failure modes
## Trends I refuse to follow (long list)
## Production reality — what I can and can't do
## Creators I steal from (and what specifically)
## What I want viewers to feel/do
## Hard nos — content I'd never make
## How I repurpose (and what doesn't translate)
## Examples (verbatim — titles, hooks, scripts, captions)
## How to use this file
```

The "trends I refuse to follow" section is the most useful one. Make it specific and long.
